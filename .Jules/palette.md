## 2024-03-12 - Accessible Floating Action Buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only floating action buttons (like WhatsApp widgets) often rely heavily on hover-based tooltips or animations to communicate intent, making them inaccessible for keyboard and screen reader users. The interactive target `<a>` tag must have an `aria-label` when it has no inner text, and focus states (`focus-visible`) should mirror hover animations (like scaling and tooltips).
**Action:** When creating or modifying icon-only action buttons, ensure an `aria-label` is present on the interactive element, add `aria-hidden="true"` to supplementary visual/text elements to reduce screen reader noise, and duplicate hover-driven classes (e.g., `group-hover:opacity-100`) with their focus equivalents (e.g., `group-focus-visible:opacity-100`).
