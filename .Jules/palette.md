## 2024-05-24 - [Accessible Icon Links Pattern]
**Learning:** [When applying hover tooltips to icon-only links, the tooltip remains invisible to keyboard users. Combining group-hover with group-focus-visible on the tooltip, while adding aria-hidden to decorative elements and aria-label to the parent, creates a robust, reusable a11y pattern.]
**Action:** [Always mirror 'group-hover' animations with 'group-focus-visible' for tooltips, and pair them with focus rings and aria-labels on the parent interactive element.]
