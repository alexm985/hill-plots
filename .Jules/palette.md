## 2025-03-09 - Accessible Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** Found several icon-only buttons (like those in Hero navigation and social media links) that are missing `aria-label`s or appropriate screen reader text. This hinders accessibility for users relying on assistive technologies.
**Action:** Always verify that buttons or links containing only icons (`<i>` tags without inner text) include descriptive `aria-label` attributes.
