## 2026-03-06 - Disconnected Form Labels\n**Learning:** Form components (like the AreaCalculator) were lacking explicit `htmlFor` and `id` linkages, leading to poor screen-reader accessibility and preventing mobile tap-to-focus on labels.\n**Action:** Always ensure `<label>` elements have an `htmlFor` that matches the corresponding input's `id` attribute. Add `cursor-pointer` to the labels to make the functionality apparent.
