## 2025-03-01 - Interactive Element Focus Patterns
 **Learning:** In this project, interactive elements often contain FontAwesome icons that require `aria-hidden="true"` while their parent elements need robust `aria-label`s. Furthermore, custom tooltip classes that rely on `group-hover` (like on the WhatsApp floating button) should be mapped with `group-focus-visible` to remain accessible to keyboard users. Round buttons also need border radius explicitly declared (like `rounded-full`) for `focus-visible:ring-2` to appear nicely formatted.
 **Action:** For all future icons or icon-only buttons, systematically apply `aria-hidden="true"` to inner components, ensure parents have `aria-label`, and align tooltip/hover styles to `group-focus-visible` while establishing the proper border radius on the wrapper element.
